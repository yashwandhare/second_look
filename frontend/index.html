A second look at your reasoning

Don’t ask AI what to decide. Ask it what you might be missing.

Describe a decision and the reasons you are leaning that way. You get back the assumptions you have not checked, the factors you have not mentioned, and the places your reasoning disagrees with itself.

Every finding comes back as a question. There is no recommendation, no score, and no “best option”.

Say it the way you would say it to a friend

Mention everything you are weighing, even the small things. The audit measures how much attention each factor gets.
